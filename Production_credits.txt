CURRENT INFOGRAPHIC LAYOUT
The user-supplied Snakemake workflow graph is the current layout reference.
Its green, cyan, yellow and red outline colours are used in the R code.
The process boxes and arrows are drawn by R/grid. Snakemake runs the file
builds and input checks. No Graphviz renderer or external diagram artwork
is embedded in the infographic. The supplied example's original designer
was not identified, so no individual designer is named.

POTASH HISTORY AND RESEARCH
The full content source list is in references.tsv and the written PDF.
Samuel Hopkins is credited for an early potash-manufacturing improvement
patented in 1790, supported by the USPTO. This is not the modern KCl process.
Kalium's team built the Belle Plaine solution mine in 1964, supported by Mosaic.
Murray Schultz is credited for the teaching activity, and Larry Bogdan for
the earlier lesson plan adapted by the Saskatchewan Mining Association. They are not presented as inventors
of modern solution mining. The cited sources identify no single inventor of
the whole modern process.
